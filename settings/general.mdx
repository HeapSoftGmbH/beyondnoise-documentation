title: "General Settings"
description: "Manage your workspace name and website URL"
---

General settings let you update your workspace's name and website URL.

## Updating your workspace

1. Navigate to your workspace.
2. Click **Settings** in the sidebar.
3. Update the **Name** field.
4. Update the **Website** field.
5. Click **Update Workspace** to save.

<Note>
  The workspace name appears in the sidebar, breadcrumbs, and workspace switcher. The website URL is used for favicon display and automatic brand context generation.
</Note>
