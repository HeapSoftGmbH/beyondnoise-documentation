---
title: "Billing"
description: "View your subscription plan, manage billing, and download invoices"
---

Billing settings show your current subscription plan and let you manage it through the Stripe billing portal.

## Viewing your plan

1. Navigate to your workspace.
2. Click **Billing** in the sidebar.
3. You'll see your current plan name, price, and status.

## Managing your plan

Click **Manage Plan** to open the billing portal. From there you can:

- Change your subscription plan
- Update payment methods
- Cancel your subscription
- View billing history

<Warning>
  Changes made in the Stripe portal take effect immediately. Plan downgrades may prorate.
</Warning>

## Invoices

The invoices table shows your past payments with date and amount. Click **View** to download or view an invoice in Stripe.

## Upgrading plans

To upgrade from Solo to Team:

1. Click **New Workspace** on the workspaces page.
2. The plan selection screen will show your current Solo plan.
3. Click **Next** on the Team plan to upgrade.

<Info>
  The Team plan unlocks daily continuous research schedules and multiple workspaces.
</Info>
