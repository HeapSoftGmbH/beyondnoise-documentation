Usage settings show a breakdown of your AI research costs across ad-hoc and continuous research.

## Cost chart

The cost chart displays a stacked bar chart showing monthly costs split by:

- **Research** - Ad-hoc research query costs
- **Continuous Research** - Continuous research run costs

Use the month switcher to navigate between months. The chart covers the selected month with daily granularity.

## Cost history table

Below the chart, a table lists individual research runs with:

- **Date** - When the research was executed
- **Type** - Research or Continuous Research
- **Prompt** - The question that was asked
- **Cost** - The AI cost for that run (in USD)

<Note>
  Costs are tracked per workspace and reflect actual AI API usage including model tokens and web search calls.
</Note>

## Tips for managing costs

- Use **Auto** or **Minimal** depth for quick lookups.
- Reserve **High** and **Extra High** depth for important strategic questions.
- Deactivate continuous research topics you no longer need.
- Consider weekly or monthly schedules instead of daily when possible.
