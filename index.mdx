BeyondNoise is a competitive intelligence platform that helps you stay ahead of the noise. It continuously monitors data sources, answers strategic questions, and alerts you when something changes in your competitive landscape.

## What can you do with BeyondNoise?

- **Research** — Ask any competitive intelligence question and get an AI-powered answer backed by web sources with citations.
- **Continuous Research** — Set up recurring research that runs on a schedule (daily, weekly, or monthly) and tracks how answers evolve over time.
- **Brand Context** — Define your brand's context so research results are tailored to your business perspective.
- **Workspaces** — Organize your intelligence work into separate workspaces for different products or teams.
- **Usage Tracking** — Monitor your AI research costs with per-query breakdowns and monthly charts.

## Getting started

1. **Sign in** — Use Google OAuth or email \+ OTP to create your account.
2. **Create a workspace** — Set up your first workspace with a name and website URL.
3. **Add brand context** — Optionally provide context about your brand so research is tailored to your perspective.
4. **Choose a plan** — Select the Solo or Team plan based on your needs.
5. **Start researching** — Run ad-hoc research or set up continuous monitoring.

Head to the [Authentication](/authentication) page to get started, or jump straight into [Research](/research).
