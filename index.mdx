BeyondNoise is a competitive intelligence platform that helps you stay ahead of the noise. It continuously monitors data sources, answers strategic questions, and alerts you when something changes in your competitive landscape.

## What can you do with BeyondNoise?

- **Research** — Ask any competitive intelligence question and get an AI-powered answer backed by web sources with citations.
- **Continuous Research** — Set up recurring research that runs on a schedule (daily, weekly, or monthly) and tracks how answers evolve over time.

Head to the [Authentication](/authentication) page to get started, or jump straight into [Research](/research).
