title: "Authentication"
description: "Sign in to BeyondNoise with Google OAuth or email + OTP"
---

BeyondNoise supports two sign-in methods: Google OAuth and email with one-time passcode (OTP).

## Sign in with Google

1. Navigate to the [login page](https://beyondnoise.io/auth/login).
2. Click **Sign in with Google**.
3. Complete the Google OAuth flow in the popup window.
4. You'll be redirected back to BeyondNoise automatically.

## Sign in with email

1. Navigate to the [login page](https://beyondnoise.io/auth/login).
2. Enter your email address in the email field below the OAuth buttons.
3. Click **Send code**.
4. Check your inbox for a 6-digit one-time passcode.
5. Enter the code in the OTP input.
6. You'll be logged in and redirected to your workspaces.

<Note>
  The OTP is valid for a limited time. If it expires, request a new code.
</Note>

## Session management

BeyondNoise uses JWT-validated sessions via Supabase Auth. Your session persists across browser restarts. To log out, click the **Logout** button on the workspaces page.

## Authentication for API access

BeyondNoise supports OAuth 2.0 consent flows for third-party applications. When an external app requests access to your account, you'll see a consent screen listing the requested scopes. You can approve or deny the request.
