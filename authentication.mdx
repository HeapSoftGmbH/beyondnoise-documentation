---
title: "Authentication"
description: "Sign in to BeyondNoise with Google OAuth or email"
---

BeyondNoise supports two sign-in methods: Google OAuth and email.

## Sign in with Google

1. Navigate to the [login page](https://app.beyondnoise.io/auth/login).
2. Click **Sign in with Google**.
3. Complete the Google OAuth flow in the popup window.
4. You'll be redirected back to BeyondNoise automatically.

## Sign in with email

1. Navigate to the [login page](https://app.beyondnoise.io/auth/login).
2. Enter your email address in the email field
3. Use your OTP for sign in.

<Note>
  The OTP is valid for a limited time. If it expires, request a new code.
</Note>
