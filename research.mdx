---

Ad-hoc research lets you ask any competitive intelligence question and get an AI-powered answer backed by web sources.

## Creating a research query

1. Navigate to your workspace and click **Research** in the sidebar.
2. Type your question in the input field.
3. Optionally configure the settings.
4. Click **Send** to start the research.

## Research settings

### Brand context

Toggle the **Brand context** switch to include your workspace's brand context in the research. This tailors the AI's answer to your business perspective.

### Research depth

Choose how deep the AI should research:

| Level | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Auto | Automatically selects the best depth |
| Minimal | Fastest, lightweight scan |
| Low | Quick scan with some depth |
| Medium | Balanced depth and speed |
| High | Thorough research |
| Extra High | Most comprehensive, slowest |

<Tip>
  Higher depth take longer to run but produce more thorough answers.
</Tip>

## Viewing results

The interface splits into two panels:

- **Left panel** - Chat conversation with your question and the AI's answer.
- **Right panel** - Source citations showing the web sources used.

## Past research

All past research is listed in the sidebar. Click any item to revisit it. Delete by hovering and clicking the trash icon.
