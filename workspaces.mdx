Workspaces are the top-level organizing unit in BeyondNoise. Each workspace represents a product, brand, or team and contains its own research, continuous research topics, brand context, and settings.

## Creating a workspace

New workspaces are created during the [onboarding flow](/onboarding):

1. Click **New Workspace** on the workspaces page.
2. Enter a **workspace name** (e.g., your product or company name).
3. Enter a **website URL** (optional, but recommended - it enables automatic brand context generation).
4. Proceed to brand context and plan selection.

## Switching workspaces

After signing in, you'll see all your workspaces on the workspaces page. Click any workspace card to enter it. The sidebar shows a workspace switcher at the top for quick navigation between workspaces.

## Workspace dashboard

When you enter a workspace, you land on the Continuous Research dashboard. This shows:

- All active and inactive continuous research topics
- Their schedules (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Last run and next scheduled run dates
- Quick links to create new research or run ad-hoc research

## Managing workspaces

Each workspace has its own settings accessible from the sidebar:

- [General settings](/settings/general) - Rename the workspace or update its website URL
- [Brand Context](/brand-context) - Edit the brand context used in research
- [Billing](/settings/billing) - View plan and manage subscription
- [Usage](/settings/usage) - Track AI research costs

<Note>
  Workspaces are isolated. Research, brand context, and billing are scoped per workspace.
</Note>
