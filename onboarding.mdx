The onboarding flow guides you through creating your first workspace, adding brand context, selecting a plan, and completing payment.

## Steps

### 1. Create a workspace

Enter a workspace name and an optional website URL. If you provide a URL, BeyondNoise can automatically generate brand context from it.

### 2. Brand context

If you provided a website URL, BeyondNoise automatically researches your brand and generates a brand context summary. You can review and edit it in the rich text editor before continuing.

If you skip the website URL, you'll need to enter brand context manually.

<Info>
  Brand context helps tailor research results to your business perspective. You can always edit it later in [Brand Context settings](/brand-context).
</Info>

### 3. Choose a plan

Choose between the **Solo** and **Team** plans:

| Feature | Solo | Team |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad-hoc research | Yes | Yes |
| Continuous research (weekly/monthly) | Yes | Yes |
| Daily continuous research | No | Yes |
| Multiple workspaces | No | Yes |

## Resuming onboarding

If you close the browser during onboarding, BeyondNoise remembers your progress. When you return, you'll resume from the step where you left off.
