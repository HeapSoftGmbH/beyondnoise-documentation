Competitor tracking runs automatically once per day and builds a table of your direct and indirect competitors, including your own company for benchmarking. There's nothing to set up, every workspace with a website URL is tracked.

## How it works

Every day, BeyondNoise researches your workspace's website and finds:

- **Direct competitors** - companies offering the same product to the same audience.
- **Indirect competitors** - companies with a different product that fully or partly solves the same problem.

For each company, the run collects:

- Name and website
- A short description
- How they overlap with you
- Employee count
- Funding stage
- HQ location
- Monthly website visits

Your [brand context](/brand-context) is included in every run, so results reflect your business perspective.

## Requirements

Competitor tracking needs a website URL for your workspace. Set it under **Settings → Website**. Workspaces without a website URL are skipped.

## Viewing competitors

Open **Competitors** in your workspace sidebar. The table shows the latest run within the current month, with a badge showing how many hours ago the data was collected.

- **Sort** any column by clicking its header.
- **Click a row** to open the detail panel with the full profile for that company.
- Click a company's **name or website** to visit their site directly.
